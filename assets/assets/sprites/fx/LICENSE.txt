Battle move-impact effect sprites
=================================

Source: Pokémon Showdown client, `play.pokemonshowdown.com/fx/`
        https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
         http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The Showdown client states (header of `play.pokemonshowdown.com/src/
battle-animations.ts`) that the CC0 dedication "DOES extend to all images in
the fx/ folder, with the exception of icicle.png, lightning.png, and bone.png"
plus `rocks.png` / `rock1.png` / `rock2.png` (GPLv3, by PO user "Gilad").

None of those exceptions are bundled here — every file in this directory is
CC0, so the app carries no copyleft obligation from them.

Files: bottombite, feather, fist, impact, leftslash, petal, rightslash, shine,
       topbite, wisp
